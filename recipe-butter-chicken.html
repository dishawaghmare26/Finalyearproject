Butter Chicken

A rich, creamy, and flavorful Indian curry made with tender chicken simmered in a spiced tomato gravy. A classic favorite from North India.

Medium
50 mins
4 Servings

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs (900g) boneless, skinless chicken breast or thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp butter or ghee
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cups tomato purée
  • 1 cup heavy cream or cashew paste
  • 1 tbsp sugar or honey
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, mix the chicken pieces with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, half of the garam masala, turmeric powder, chili powder, and lemon juice. Marinate for at least 30 minutes, or for best results, overnight in the refrigerator.
  2. Melt butter or ghee in a large pan or pot over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook until soft and translucent.
  3. Add the marinated chicken to the pan and cook until it is seared on all sides, about 5-7 minutes.
  4. Pour in the tomato purée, remaining garam masala, and sugar or honey. Stir well. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and let it cook for 20-25 minutes, or until the chicken is tender and cooked through.
  5. Stir in the heavy cream or cashew paste. Season with salt to taste. Gently simmer for another 5 minutes, allowing the sauce to thicken slightly.
  6. Serve hot with basmati rice, naan bread, or roti. Garnish with fresh cilantro if desired.
