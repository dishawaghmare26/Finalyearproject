10 Time-Saving Kitchen Hacks Every Home Cook Should Know

By The DesiEats Team | September 23, 2025 | Category: Tips & Tricks

In today's fast-paced world, finding time to cook can be a challenge. But with a few clever tricks up your sleeve, you can cut down on prep time and make cooking more efficient. Here are 10 time-saving kitchen hacks that every home cook should know.

1. The Blender Trick for Onion & Garlic Paste

Ditch the time-consuming process of finely chopping onions and garlic. Instead, roughly chop them and toss them in a blender with a little water. You'll have a smooth paste in seconds, perfect for gravies and curries.

2. Freeze Your Herbs

Don't let fresh herbs go to waste. Chop them finely and place them in an ice cube tray. Pour olive oil or melted butter over them and freeze. Pop a cube into your pan when cooking for instant flavor.

3. Use a Ziploc Bag to Crush Spices

Need to crush whole spices or crackers? Place them in a Ziploc bag, seal it, and roll over it with a rolling pin. It's less messy than using a mortar and pestle and gets the job done quickly.

4. The Microwave for Soaking Lentils

Forgot to soak your lentils overnight? No problem. Place the lentils in a microwave-safe bowl with three times the amount of water. Microwave for 10-15 minutes, and they'll be soft and ready to cook.

5. Batch-Prep Your Ginger-Garlic Paste

Ginger-garlic paste is a staple in many dishes. Make a large batch once a week by blending peeled ginger and garlic with a little oil. Store it in an airtight container in the fridge for easy access.

6. The Squeeze Bottle for Pancakes

For mess-free pancakes and waffles, prepare your batter and pour it into a clean, empty squeeze bottle. It makes pouring precise and easy, and you can store any leftover batter in the fridge.

7. Peel Potatoes in Seconds

After boiling your potatoes, immediately transfer them to an ice bath. The skin will loosen and become very easy to peel off with just your hands.

8. Cut Cherry Tomatoes with a Plate

To slice multiple cherry tomatoes at once, place a handful of them between two small plastic lids or plates. Hold the top plate firmly and slice horizontally through the middle of the tomatoes with a sharp knife.

9. Organize Your Spices

An organized spice rack saves a lot of time. Arrange your spices alphabetically or by cuisine so you can find what you need in a flash. You can even use small labeled jars for consistency.

10. The Instant Marinade Bag

Instead of marinating in a bowl, use a Ziploc bag. It reduces cleanup and allows the marinade to coat the meat evenly with less liquid. Just toss, seal, and let it sit!

Incorporating these simple kitchen hacks into your routine will make cooking a more relaxed and enjoyable experience. Happy cooking!
